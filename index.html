The Standard

Most small homes are caravans in disguise — unapprovable, uninsurable, unfinanceable. We build the other kind: certified permanence.

01 / FIRE

Forged, not painted

Yakisugi — timber charred over open flame until the surface carbonises. It seals against moisture, resists insects, and weathers for decades. No coatings. No chemicals.

02 / PAPER

Certified permanence

NCC Class 1A — the same classification as any house on any street. Council-approvable, insurable as residential property, legally rentable. The certification pack travels with the home.

03 / STEEL

Delivered, not promised

One rigid BlueScope steel structure, built in our Queensland factory and delivered complete — engineered for cyclonic wind regions, connected by licensed trades.
