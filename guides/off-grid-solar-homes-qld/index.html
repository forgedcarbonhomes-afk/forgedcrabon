Solar-ready as standard

A home built to make its own power.

Every Forge 12 leaves our workshop solar-ready: a Colorbond roof suited to panels and a fully AS/NZS 3000 compliant electrical system. That means adding solar is straightforward — not a retrofit headache.

From there, solar is an optional add-on package. We supply and coordinate the panels — and battery storage if you want it — sized to how you'll use the home. Whether you're cutting power bills on a suburban block or going fully off-grid on acreage, the home is ready for it.

Off-grid, the right way

Self-sufficient — and still a real home.

Plenty of "off-grid cabins" are really just sheds or caravans with panels bolted on — which leaves you with the same problem: they can't be council-approved, insured or rented as a home. The Forge 12 is different. It's certified NCC Class 1A, so you get genuine off-grid independence in a dwelling that's still a permanent, approvable, insurable home.

That makes it ideal for remote and rural Central Queensland land where mains power is expensive or unavailable — a self-sufficient home on acreage, a solar-run granny flat, or an off-grid Airbnb. See the cost breakdown or explore the ways people use it.
