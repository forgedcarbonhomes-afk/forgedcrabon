Why a "granny flat price" is never one number

The home is fixed. Your site isn't.

The price of the home itself is fixed and transparent — $149,000 for a complete, certified Forge 12. What changes from one project to the next is the site: how far it has to be delivered, what your ground requires for footings, how far services have to run, and your council's fees. That's true of every modular home and granny flat in Queensland, whoever builds it.

Rather than hide those costs in a single "from" number, we quote them separately and itemised — so you can compare like for like and there are no surprises at delivery.

The other side of the ledger

A cost that can pay itself back

Because the Forge 12 is a Class 1A dwelling, it can be legally rented — short-stay or long-term — and it adds permanent, appreciating value to your land rather than depreciating like a caravan. For many owners that turns the purchase into an income-producing asset. Model your own numbers on the calculator.
