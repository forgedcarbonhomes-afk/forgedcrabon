Kitchenette Finish Levels

Compact doesn’t mean basic

The standard Forge 12 kitchenette is oak-finish cabinetry with a quartz benchtop. If you want a different material or hand-finish level, our Rockhampton cabinetry partner Bilt & Co can spec and install it to the same footprint — quoted separately to the display price.

Essence

Handleless soft-matte doors on Blum soft-close runners, stone benchtop. The Forge 12 standard, one level up.

Maison

Timber veneer against a full-height stone splashback, with integrated lighting scaled to the kitchenette run.

Atelier

Book-matched stone, solid brass hardware, curved cabinetry — the same detailing as their largest builds, fitted to 3.5m.
