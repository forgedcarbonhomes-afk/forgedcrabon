Imported, not just cheap

We source it, we supply it.

Forged Carbon built its name on certified modular homes. We're extending that same standard to building materials — kitchens, bathroom fixtures, and general building supplies, sourced internationally and supplied direct for your own project, whatever it is.

"Imported" doesn't mean "uncertified." Any plumbing fixture we supply carries WaterMark certification, and any electrical item carries SAA/RCM approval — that's a legal requirement in Australia regardless of where a product is made, and we don't treat it as optional.
