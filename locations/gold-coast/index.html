Council approval in Surfers Paradise

Approvable under Gold Coast City Council

Secondary dwellings and small homes in Surfers Paradise are assessed under Gold Coast City Council's planning scheme. Because the Forge 12 is certified NCC Class 1A — the same building classification as a standard house — it can be lodged for approval as a secondary or primary dwelling through a private building certifier, insured as residential property, and legally rented.

With land values this high, an uncertified 'granny flat' that can't be insured or legally let is a genuinely expensive mistake. A Class 1A dwelling can be rented short-stay through the Gold Coast's huge holiday market or long-term — the certification is what makes either option legal in the first place.

Check current local requirements at the official Gold Coast City Council website, or ask us — we work with private certifiers on lodgement and supply the full certification pack with every home.

Surfers Paradise FAQ

Common questions

How much does a granny flat cost in Surfers Paradise?

The Forge 12 is $149,000 AUD as displayed — a complete NCC Class 1A certified dwelling. Delivery to Surfers Paradise (approx. 700 km by road from our Rockhampton display) and site connections are quoted separately. Contact us for an all-in estimate for your block.

Are tiny homes council approved in Surfers Paradise?

Only if they are certified as a permanent dwelling. Most tiny homes and caravan-style units cannot be approved as housing. The Forge 12 is NCC Class 1A — the same classification as a standard house — so it can be lodged for approval under Gold Coast City Council's planning scheme via a private building certifier.

Is the Forge 12 a kit home?

No — a traditional kit home ships as components for you or a builder to assemble on site. The Forge 12 is built complete at our Queensland factory and delivered whole to your Surfers Paradise block, already certified, which is why it can be insured and financed like a standard house.

Can I put a container or foldable home on my block in Surfers Paradise?

Shipping-container and fold-out homes usually aren't certified as Class 1A dwellings, which makes council approval, insurance and finance difficult in the Gold Coast City Council area. A certified permanent dwelling avoids those problems — see our container homes guide for the full comparison.

Is the Forge 12 suitable for Surfers Paradise's climate?

Yes. The Forge 12's BlueScope steel frame is engineered to the AS/NZS 1170.2 wind rating confirmed for your specific Surfers Paradise site during approval, with Shou Sugi Ban charred-timber cladding, R4.0 roof / R2.5 wall insulation, cedar windows and Colorbond roofing built for the Queensland climate.
