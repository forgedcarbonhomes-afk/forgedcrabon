Council approval in Maroochydore

Approvable under Sunshine Coast Regional Council

Secondary dwellings and small homes in Maroochydore are assessed under Sunshine Coast Regional Council's planning scheme. Because the Forge 12 is certified NCC Class 1A — the same building classification as a standard house — it can be lodged for approval as a secondary or primary dwelling through a private building certifier, insured as residential property, and legally rented.

Growth here is being driven by both lifestyle migration and the new Sunshine Coast University Hospital precinct, which means more households looking for flexible living arrangements — multigenerational family setups or rental income — on an existing block, without the cost and time of a full second house.

Check current local requirements at the official Sunshine Coast Regional Council website, or ask us — we work with private certifiers on lodgement and supply the full certification pack with every home.

Maroochydore FAQ

Common questions

How much does a granny flat cost in Maroochydore?

The Forge 12 is $149,000 AUD as displayed — a complete NCC Class 1A certified dwelling. Delivery to Maroochydore (approx. 490 km by road from our Rockhampton display) and site connections are quoted separately. Contact us for an all-in estimate for your block.

Are tiny homes council approved in Maroochydore?

Only if they are certified as a permanent dwelling. Most tiny homes and caravan-style units cannot be approved as housing. The Forge 12 is NCC Class 1A — the same classification as a standard house — so it can be lodged for approval under Sunshine Coast Regional Council's planning scheme via a private building certifier.

Is the Forge 12 a kit home?

No — a traditional kit home ships as components for you or a builder to assemble on site. The Forge 12 is built complete at our Queensland factory and delivered whole to your Maroochydore block, already certified, which is why it can be insured and financed like a standard house.

Can I put a container or foldable home on my block in Maroochydore?

Shipping-container and fold-out homes usually aren't certified as Class 1A dwellings, which makes council approval, insurance and finance difficult in the Sunshine Coast Regional Council area. A certified permanent dwelling avoids those problems — see our container homes guide for the full comparison.

Is the Forge 12 suitable for Maroochydore's climate?

Yes. The Forge 12's BlueScope steel frame is engineered to the AS/NZS 1170.2 wind rating confirmed for your specific Maroochydore site during approval, with Shou Sugi Ban charred-timber cladding, R4.0 roof / R2.5 wall insulation, cedar windows and Colorbond roofing built for the Queensland climate.
